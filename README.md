# Who Will Build Australia's Future? STEM education from classroom to career

FIT3179 Data Visualisation 2 (Semester 2, 2026) – Lim Hui Zern

Live page: https://hzern.github.io/fit3179-dv2/

- `index.html` – the visualisation page
- `css/style.css` – layout and typography
- `js/*.vl.json` – Vega-Lite specifications for all 11 charts (human-readable JSON)
- `data/` – CSV and TopoJSON data used by the charts
- `sketch/` – PDF of the hand-drawn A4 sketch

Data: Australian Government Department of Education (Higher Education Statistics 2024), Department of Industry, Science and Resources (STEM Equity Monitor 2026), OECD (PISA 2022). See the footer of the page for full sources.
